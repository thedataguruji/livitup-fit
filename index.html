III

The Practice

Three disciplines. One body.

Yoga, rehabilitation, and nutrition aren't sold separately here — they're one plan, built around what your body actually needs.

Yoga

Ashtanga, Yin, functional movement, and Face Yoga — rooted in a single consistent lineage, not a rotating menu of styles.

Therapy

Clinical rehabilitation for the spine, knee, shoulder, and nervous system — yoga used as medicine, not metaphor.

Nutrition

A yogic framework for eating, translated through modern nutrition science — not another diet to follow and abandon.

Eating is part of the practice.

Yogic nutrition isn't a meal plan — it's a framework. We draw on ritucharya, the practice of eating with the season, and sattvic principles, then translate both through a modern, evidence-based lens: real food, right portions, and habits built to outlast any 30-day challenge.
